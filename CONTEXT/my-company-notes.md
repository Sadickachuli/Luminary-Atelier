# For my-company.md

Raw material from the interview — brand facts and positioning to fold into the company file.

- **Two complementary brands, one belief.** Celebrate Sweetly (corporate gifting and relationship marketing) and Luminary Atelier (gatherings, experiences, human connection) both stand for **gratitude through thoughtfulness.** Celebrate Sweetly is the *tangible* — the object that proves you listened. Luminary Atelier is the *intangible* — the experience and connection in the room.
- **Core philosophy:** personalization over "one size fits all." Do the homework, know the recipient, let the gift reflect something true about them. A personal touch is possible even in a large group gift.
- **Signature proof point:** the tiny frog story — a small, specific detail that proved someone was listening, and became the thing the recipient remembered most.
- **Aesthetic = quiet luxury through restraint.** Solid satin or grosgrain ribbon over bright patterns; the meaningful detail over fluff and filler. No padding a box to fake its size.
- **Ideal client:** someone who treats gratitude as a natural part of being, not just doing business.
- **Not for:** anyone who sees appreciation as optional, or who gives out of obligation ("we have to say thank you for the holidays") rather than genuine desire.
- **The fault line for the whole brand:** *want to* vs. *have to.* Genuine appreciation, never obligation.
- **September relaunch — the headline:** pull *human connection* to the front. Not a new product line; a shift in emphasis. Luminary Atelier makes connection the experience; Celebrate Sweetly's tangible gifts become *even more* thoughtful as more time goes into learning the recipient.
- **Positioning anchor (priority):** **gratitude through connection is the one thing AI cannot do.** As the industry automates gifting, Merley does the opposite — the human listening is the product. The frog is the proof: a machine doesn't catch the frog; a person who's listening does.
