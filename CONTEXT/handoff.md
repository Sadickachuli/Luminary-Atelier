## Decisions (and why)

- **Luminary gets its own standalone site, not a page inside Shopify.** A Shopify store carries only one web address, so a second domain would redirect to celebratesweetly.com and the gathering pages would sit inside a shop with cart and product menus. The lead brand needs its own name and no commerce furniture.
- **Celebrate Sweetly stays on Shopify.** Her Studio theme is modern and section-based, so no custom Liquid is needed for the refresh.
- **Engagement changed from coaching to build.** Across five coaching sessions nothing shipped, because the hours went into screen sharing, finding buttons and troubleshooting. She agreed on a call that Sadick should build it.
- **$35 per hour, 49.5 estimated hours.** $1,732.50 less a $90 credit and a $200 discount, so $1,442.50. Three installments: $500 to start, $500 at her review of Luminary, $442.50 at launch.
- **The site must be editable by her where possible,** rather than her using Claude to make changes. The coaching sessions showed she gets stuck on interfaces.
- **No options presented in the final proposal.** She had already chosen the build on a call, so offering a menu would have reopened a settled decision.

## Dead ends, do not retry

- **luminaryatelier.com is not hers.** It belongs to someone else and shows a Squarespace holding page. Her domains are theluminaryatelier.com, .net, .info, .store and .shop. The proposal still says luminaryatelier.com and needs correcting with her.
- **Her Squarespace is a red herring.** There was never a Squarespace site of hers. No access needed.
- **GoDaddy's website builder cannot be repurposed.** Her two paid Websites and Marketing Starter plans are on micaricollective.com and camiari.com. Luminary only has the free tier, which cannot carry a real site on a custom domain.
- **Do not build a custom CMS.** Rejected as expensive to build, a permanent maintenance burden, and it would leave her dependent on Sadick forever.
- **Do not use her LinkedIn posts as voice samples.** She confirmed they are reposts and quote graphics, not her voice. Her gift notes and client emails are the real samples.
- **A second Shopify store was rejected,** at about $39 a month, because there is no confirmed need for ticketing or payments for gatherings.
