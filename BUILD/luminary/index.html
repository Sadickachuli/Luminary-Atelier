[ 01 ] Philosophy

Someone was listening.

She mentioned it once, almost in passing. She loved tiny frogs.

So when she moved into her new home, tucked in with her gift was a tiny ceramic frog. When her thank-you note came, the frog was what she wrote about. Not because it was precious, but because it told her someone had heard her.

That is what Luminary Atelier is for. Celebrate Sweetly gives the listening something to hold. Luminary gives it a room.

We gather fifteen to twenty people, phones put away, and give the time to a conversation about gratitude, guided so that every person in the room is heard.

So much of how we say thank you has become automatic. Here, the listening is the whole point, and it is never rushed.

This is for people who say thank you because they want to, not because the calendar says they should. If that sounds like you, I would love to hear what you have in mind.

[ 03 ] About Merley

Why I listen first

I have spent about fourteen years helping people say thank you through Celebrate Sweetly, from my home in Los Angeles.

In that time I have heard more than one story of a gift chosen without listening. Steaks sent to someone who is vegetarian. Alcohol sent to someone in recovery. No one meant any harm. They simply had not done the homework.

The homework is the part I love. Knowing not just that someone reads, but what they read. That is where a personal touch comes from, even in a gift for fifty people.

Over the years I came to see that the gift was only ever half of it. The other half is being in a room together, giving each person your full attention, and letting them feel it.

That is why I named this Luminary Atelier. A luminary gives off light and helps others find their way. An atelier is a workshop, a small studio where things are made with care. I want this to be both: a place to learn the art of gratitude, and a place to practice it together.

If you would like to learn it with me, I would be honored to guide you.
